# Chinese Meat Dumplings
This recipe is for making delicious Chinese meat dumplings. It requires some preparation time and patience, but the result is worth it. 

This recipe makes approximately 60 dumplings, enough to feed 6-8 people or 4 very hungry people.

## Ingredients
- 500g of 00 flour (T45) + 100g for dusting
- 400g of pork (or beef)
- 400g of Chinese cabbage (or regular cabbage, or savoy cabbage)
- 50g of scallions (or leek, or half an onion)
- 3 slices of ginger
- 0.3l of soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons of salt
- ~0.3 l of water
- ~15 ml (1 tablespoon) of rice wine (optional)
- ~15 ml (1 tablespoon) of sesame oil (optional)
- ~2.5 g (3 turns of the grinder) of pepper
- ~2.5 g (3 turns of the grinder) of five spice powder (optional)

## Directions

1. Pour the flour into a bowl that can withstand hot water, create a well in the center, add 2 teaspoons of salt, and pour in 0.3l of boiling water. Mix until it reaches a smooth and soft consistency, adding more flour if necessary. Let it **rest** for 30 minutes.
2. Finely chop the meat, cabbage, scallions, and ginger. Mix everything with 4 tablespoons of soy sauce, pepper, five spice powder, rice wine, and sesame oil. Let the filling **rest** in the fridge until it's time to assemble the dumplings.
3. Dust the work surface with flour, take a handful of dough, and roll it into a sausage shape about 2 cm in diameter. Cut into 1 cm thick cylinders. Use a rolling pin to flatten each piece into a 2 mm thick disc. Place 2 teaspoons of filling in the center of each disc and fold into a half-moon shape, sealing the edges.
4. To pan-fry, heat a pan and brown the dumplings for a couple of minutes. Add 1 cup of water, bring to a boil, and cook covered on low heat for 12 minutes. Alternatively, steam the dumplings for 15 minutes with the lid on over low heat.
5. Serve the dumplings hot, accompanied by soy sauce.

Enjoy your homemade Chinese meat dumplings!
